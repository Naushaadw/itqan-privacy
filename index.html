# Privacy Policy **Last updated: October 1, 2026** Itqān ("the App") is a Quran memorization and revision application developed by Naushaad Wazir. This Privacy Policy explains how Itqān handles information when you use the App. ## 1. Information We Collect Itqān does not require you to create an account and does not ask you to provide personal information such as your name, email address, telephone number, contacts, or location. The App stores certain app-related information locally on your device, such as your Mushaf page position, quiz settings, and other preferences required for the functionality of the App. The Quran text and related Quranic data used by the App are stored locally on the device and are not submitted to the developer. ## 2. How Information Is Used Information stored locally by the App is used solely to provide and maintain the functionality of Itqān, including remembering your preferences and restoring your previous position in the Mushaf. We do not use this information for advertising, profiling, or selling user data. ## 3. Data Sharing Itqān does not sell, rent, or share the locally stored app information with third parties. The App does not provide personal user information to the developer or other parties through a user account or backend service. ## 4. Data Storage and Retention App-related information is stored locally on your device. Because this information is stored locally, it generally remains on the device until it is removed by the App, cleared by the user, or the App is uninstalled, depending on the type of information. Itqān does not maintain a server-side account containing this information. ## 5. Data Security Itqān does not transmit the locally stored app information to the developer's servers. Users should keep their device and operating system appropriately secured and up to date. ## 6. Children's Privacy Itqān does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App does not require users to create an account or submit personal information, it does not intentionally collect children's personal information. ## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the App or applicable requirements. When the policy is updated, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be changed. ## 8.
